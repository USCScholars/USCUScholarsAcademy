The Scholars Academy is a dual-enrollment program on the University of South Carolina Upstate campus that allows students to take college courses while still participating in other classes or extracurricular activities at their high school. The program is based upon promoting inclusivity, learning, engagement, ambition, and achievement. In general, the Scholars Academy works to instill the values of service, leadership, and integrity in its students.
